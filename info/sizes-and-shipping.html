Print Sizes, Quality & Shipping

Every listing in our shop follows the same print standards below — digital download or physical poster, USA shipping, and archival print quality.

Digital Download

Instant access after purchase — no shipping fees, no wait, available worldwide. Print at home or through your favorite local or online print shop.

We currently ship physical posters within the USA only. If you're located outside the USA, the digital version lets you enjoy your artwork without international shipping delays.

What's Included in a Digital File Purchase

5 high-resolution printable files (300 DPI) covering the most popular frame sizes, across 5 aspect ratios:

  • 2:3 ratio — 4×6" · 8×12" · 10×15" · 12×18" · 16×24" · 20×30" · 24×36"
  • 3:4 ratio — 6×8" · 9×12" · 12×16" · 15×20" · 18×24"
  • 4:5 ratio — 4×5" · 8×10" · 12×15" · 16×20"
  • 1:1 ratio — 8×8" · 10×10" · 12×12" · 16×16" · 20×20"
  • A-Series — A4

Our highest-resolution file is approximately 108 megapixels, producing sharp, crisp prints even at the largest 24×36" size.

How You'll Receive Your Files

Enter your email in the personalization box at checkout. Once your order processes, you'll receive an email with a secure download link. Download, print at home or with a professional print shop, then frame and enjoy — no shipping wait.

Physical Poster Details

Every physical poster is professionally printed on museum-grade archival paper with a premium matte finish, using Giclée printing technology and archival pigment inks for rich, fade-resistant color. Paper weight is 175–189 g/m² depending on production partner. Indoor use only.

Printed on professional Canon large-format printers with a 12-color aqueous archival ink system on acid-free, UV-tested museum-grade paper.

Packaging

Hand-rolled in protective tissue paper. Smaller posters ship in extra-thick triangular boxes; posters 24" and larger ship in heavy-duty mailing tubes. Multiple posters are combined into one package whenever possible.

Care Instructions

For indoor use only. If dust accumulates, gently wipe the surface with a clean, dry, soft cloth.

Returns & Exchanges

Because every physical poster is printed specifically for your order, returns and exchanges aren't accepted. If your order arrives damaged or there's a printing issue, please contact us — we're happy to help find a solution.

Copyright & Personal Use

All digital artwork is provided for personal use only. You may print it for your home or as a personal gift. You may not resell, share, redistribute, upload elsewhere, use commercially, or modify the artwork for resale. Purchase does not transfer copyright ownership. Frames are not included.
